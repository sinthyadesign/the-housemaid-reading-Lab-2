The Best Thing I Have Read Lately

The best thing I have read lately is The Housemaid by Freida McFadden. It is a mystery and thriller book about a woman named Millie who gets a job working as a housemaid for a wealthy family. At first, the job seems like a good opportunity, but she soon realizes that the family has many secrets.

I enjoyed this book because it kept me interested and made me want to keep reading. There were many unexpected moments and twists throughout the story. I also liked how the author slowly revealed information about the characters instead of explaining everything at the beginning.

What I liked most about The Housemaid was the suspense. While reading it, I kept wondering what was going to happen next and which characters I could trust. The story made me want to continue reading because I wanted to find out the truth.

Overall, The Housemaid was one of the most interesting books I have read lately. I would recommend it to someone who enjoys mystery, suspense, and stories with unexpected twists.
